About Kyle

Kyle Scott Harris is a decorated United States Marine — Gunnery Sergeant, Marine Raider [Marine Forces Special Operations Command], Scout Sniper — awarded the Purple Heart and Silver Star for actions in combat. He served his country with distinction.

Kyle is currently detained at Travis County Correctional Complex in Del Valle, Texas. Family and friends have set up this page to organize letters, prayer, and public information for anyone who wants to stand alongside him.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I visit Kyle?
Visitation is coordinated through Travis County Correctional Complex. Check the county sheriff's office website for current visitation rules and scheduling.

Can I put money on Kyle's phone or commissary?
Deposits are made through ConnectNetwork / GTL, the county's contracted provider. Family members handle this on a rolling basis.

What can I do to help beyond letters?
Pray. Share the mailing address with others who know Kyle. Encourage his family. That is enough. Anything more, family will reach out.

Where do I direct legal questions or media inquiries?
All legal questions go to counsel of record. All media inquiries go to family — please do not contact Kyle directly through jail phone or mail for interviews.
