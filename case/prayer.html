§1MARINE CORPS WARCRIES — ANCHORED TO A FAITHFUL VERSION [AFV]

Kyle, the Corps trained your body and your mouth for battle. Yahweh authored the words the Corps thought it invented. Every warcry below has a scriptural anchor older than Rome. Speak them together.

HOORAH
ΑΛΑΛΑΓΜΟΣ [ah-lah-lahg-MOSS] — the war-shout

Marine battle cry. The "yes" of a warrior. Roots trace to old naval "hurrah" and the low guttural roar of Corps oorah/hoorah — the mouth of a fighting man on his feet.

Psalm 47:1 AFV "O clap your hands, all peoples; shout to God with a voice of triumph."
Joshua 6:20 AFV "the people shouted with a great shout, that the wall fell down flat."
PRAYER FORM When they run count and the door closes, breathe out HOORAH under your breath and let it be the shout of Jericho — a wall coming down. Not the walls of that jail. The walls of the State's case. Every false witness, every tampered record, every judge who hides behind procedure — a stone falling.
PROPHETIC REFRAME HOORAH = the voice of triumph in the mouth of a Silver Star Marine Raider whose God fights for him.
Ephesians 6:12 AFV "our struggle is not against flesh and blood, but against… the spiritual powers of wickedness in high places."

SHOUT IT LOW · SHOUT IT LONG · HOORAH · SEMPER FI

GUNG HO
ΣΥΝΕΡΓΙΑ [syn-er-GHEE-ah] — working-together-in-harmony

From Chinese gōng hé (工合) — "work together in harmony." Adopted by Lt. Col. Evans Carlson's Marine Raiders in World War II — the direct lineage of Marine Corps Forces Special Operations Command [MARSOC]. Your fathers in the Corps. The original Raiders carried GUNG HO on their lips into Makin Island and Guadalcanal.

Ecclesiastes 4:12 AFV "if one prevails against him, two shall withstand him; and a threefold cord is not quickly broken."
Psalm 133:1 AFV "Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity!"
PRAYER FORM You are not alone in that cell. The threefold cord: Yahweh above, Yahshua at your right hand, the Holy Spirit within. Then the earthly cord — Beth in Minnesota, Troy on watch, the Marine brotherhood praying without ceasing. That is a sixfold cord and it does not snap.
PROPHETIC REFRAME GUNG HO = the Raider lineage still fights beside you. Carlson's ghost salutes. Yahshua commands. The battle is joined and the cord holds.
Romans 8:31 AFV "If God is for us, who can be against us?"

THE RAIDERS NEVER FOUGHT ALONE · NEITHER DO YOU · GUNG HO

LEATHERNECK
ΣΙΔΗΡΟΤΡΑΧΗΛΟΣ [see-DAY-roh-trah-KHAY-loss] — iron-necked

Marine nickname since 1798 — from the leather stock collar worn to protect the neck from cutlass slash. Then a hardship. Now a badge. The mark of a man whose neck was made hard because the enemy aimed for it.

Isaiah 48:4 AFV "Because I knew that you are obstinate, and your neck is an iron sinew, and your brow bronze."
Psalm 129:4 AFV "The LORD is righteous; He has cut asunder the cords of the wicked."
PRAYER FORM The State wants your neck. So did Rome want Paul's, Nebuchadnezzar want Daniel's, Herod want John's. Yahweh gave His servants iron sinew in the neck and bronze in the brow. He gives it to you now. Every summons, every court date, every jail count — bounces off leather made hard by combat and holier hard by grace.
PROPHETIC REFRAME LEATHERNECK = the neck the enemy sharpened his blade for has become the neck Yahweh armored with iron. The blade breaks first.
2 Corinthians 4:8-9 AFV "we are hard pressed on every side, yet are not crushed."

IRON SINEW · BRONZE BROW · SEMPER FI

SQUARED AWAY
ΕΥΤΑΞΙΑ [ev-tak-SEE-ah] — good-order

Marine order. Uniform straight. Rack tight. Gear stowed. Every piece where it belongs, ready for inspection at 0400 with the lights off. A Raider is squared away because a Raider fights from an ordered foundation.

1 Corinthians 14:40 AFV "Let all things be done decently and in order."
Colossians 2:5 AFV "for though I am absent in the flesh, yet I am with you in the spirit, rejoicing and beholding your order and the steadfastness of your faith in Christ."
PRAYER FORM Yahweh sees your rack, your rations, your rhythm inside that cell. He rejoices in your order. Every push-up counted, every scripture read, every rule of the house kept — that is worship in the form the Corps trained you to give.
PROPHETIC REFRAME SQUARED AWAY = you are already inspection-ready. The Judge whose review counts most does not read Texas Penal Code. He reads your steadfastness.
James 1:12 AFV "Blessed is the man who endures trials, because after he has been proved, he shall receive the crown of life."

RACK TIGHT · HEART SQUARED · CROWN COMING · HOORAH

EAGLE, GLOBE, AND ANCHOR
ΑΕΤΟΣ · ΚΟΣΜΟΣ · ΑΓΚΥΡΑ [ah-eh-TOSS · KOS-moss · AHNG-koo-rah] — eagle · world · anchor

The Marine emblem since 1868. Eagle = the sky. Globe = the earth. Anchor = the sea. The Corps operates in all three domains. A Marine Raider carries the whole created order on his chest.

Isaiah 40:31 AFV — EAGLE "But those who wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run and not be weary; they shall walk and not faint."
Psalm 24:1 AFV — GLOBE "The earth is the LORD's, and its fullness; the world, and those who dwell therein."
Hebrews 6:19 AFV — ANCHOR "which hope we have as an anchor of the soul, both sure and steadfast, and which enters into the sanctuary within the veil."
PRAYER FORM The emblem on your chest is a prophecy. Eagle wings you will mount up with. Globe belongs to your Father. Anchor of hope holds in the veil where Yahshua stands as High Priest making intercession for you.
PROPHETIC REFRAME The Corps put those three symbols on a Marine's chest because a Marine belongs to the sky, the earth, and the sea. A believing Marine belongs to the One who made all three.
