Lethal polemic kill vector — three independent kill shots:

Kill shot one — the pillow. Texas Penal Code § 1.07(a)(17)(B) requires the instrument's manner of use to be capable of causing death or serious bodily injury. A pillow is a novel deadly-weapon theory. Under the McCain v. State, 22 South Western Reporter Third 497 (Texas Court of Criminal Appeals 2000) line, the State must prove manner-of-use sufficiency. If the finding falls, the charge drops from aggravated assault to simple assault or lower, and probation restrictions under Article 42A.054 fall with it.

Kill shot two — impede breath. § 22.01(b)(2)(B) requires proof of actual impedance of normal breathing or blood circulation. Without contemporaneous medical corroboration — emergency department records, first-responder oxygen saturation readings, photographs of petechiae or bruising — the element is not proven. Kill shot three — Franks hearing. Under Franks v. Motion to Inspect Grand Jury under Texas Code of Criminal Procedure Article 20A.204 opens the door.

Any one kill shot dismantles the felony frame. All three together dismantle the case.
