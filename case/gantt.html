AMBUSH DOCTRINE — WHY SIMULTANEOUS
1. Denial of triage. Sequential filing lets the State pick the easiest motion, kill it, and use that ruling to poison the rest. Simultaneous filing eliminates the choice.
2. Full-record posture. Every judge who receives Kyle's file sees ALL 18 motions on the docket at once. No motion reads in isolation. The pattern IS the argument: this is not one aggrieved defendant — this is a systematic State failure across two counties.
3. Response-window collision. Tex. CCP + local rules require State response inside 30 days. When 18 motions land Day 0, response calendars stack. DA either misses windows (waiver) or admits inability to answer (record).
4. Media + Whale visibility. A single D-DAY press packet lands harder than 18 trickled filings. One story. One headline. One record.
5. Federal-reserve trigger arms. M13 (§ 2241 + § 1983) ripens the moment M6 or M12 is denied. Simultaneous D-DAY means both denial paths mature on the same clock — no wasted weeks.

SIX-PHASE TIMELINE

  1. Phase A · Prep (Day −21 to Day −1): draft every motion, notarize at jail, courier to two counties, prepare press + Whale packets, coordinate standby counsel.
  2. Phase B · D-DAY strike (Day 0): simultaneous filing across Collin 199th + Travis 427th + Travis County Courts. All 12 core Kyle-facing motions + 5 pressure motions hit the record inside 24 hours. P5 letters already cleared for send.
  3. Phase C · State response window (Day 1 to Day 30): DA calendars collapse. Track waiver clocks. Every 30-day non-response = motion to compel + sanctions rider (M2/M8).
  4. Phase D · Hearings (Day 30 to Day 60): forced hearings across both counties. Speedy Trial (M6/M12) paired with prosecutorial misconduct (M5) and Franks (M11) creates dismissal cascade.
  5. Phase E · Rulings (Day 45 to Day 75): orders on record. Any denial ripens federal habeas + § 1983 posture.
  6. Phase F · Federal reserve (Day 45+): M13 fires only if state remedies denied. Habeas cause # opens; § 1983 notice letters served on Melissa PD + Travis DA in personal capacity.
