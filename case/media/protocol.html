Embargo & Exclusivity Discipline

ΣΙΩΠΗ ΩΡΑΙΑ [see-oh-PAY oh-RAH-yah] — Timely Silence
  • Embargo default: Every WHISPER-stage release goes to one journalist under a 72-hour embargo tied to a specific court event.
  • Exclusivity default: Every WHISPER-stage release grants 72-hour exclusivity to the receiving journalist. After 72 hours or after the court event fires, exclusivity ends.
  • Break clause: If the receiving journalist sits on the story past the court event, the exclusivity ends immediately and the story goes to the next Tier 1 name on the list.
  • Multi-journalist warm release: No embargo. All Tier 1 + 2 + 3 receive simultaneously via BCC, with the feature story attached and the interview offer standing.
  • No leaks to hostile outlets: If a Rogue Independent or Podcast Whale asks for the story before Stage 4, the answer is “standing offer, not yet.”
