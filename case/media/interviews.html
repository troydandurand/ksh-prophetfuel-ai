Opening Statement (60 seconds)

KYLE “Thanks for taking the call. I am United States Marine Corps Gunnery Sergeant Kyle Scott Harris. Marine Raider under Marine Corps Forces Special Operations Command. Scout Sniper. Purple Heart. Silver Star. I am being held pretrial at the Travis County Correctional Complex in Texas. I have not been to trial. I have never been convicted of the charges I am being held on. I am asking for the right to represent myself under Faretta v. California. That is what this call is about. Thank you.”

Five Talking Points

  • 1. I served this country in combat. I hold the Purple Heart and the Silver Star. I earned the right to speak on the record.
  • 2. I have not been to trial. The complainant is my accuser. The star witness against me has now been credibly identified as the actual tamperer in this case. I have documentary proof.
  • 3. The judge denied the complainant her own protective order request. Read that again. The judge denied her.
  • 4. I am being held with a tablet that does not run desktop legal research and does not save my work session to session. That violates Bounds v. Smith.
  • 5. I want a trial. I have wanted a trial for more than a year. The record shows every speedy-trial demand I have filed.

Ten Hostile Counter-Questions

Q1 — HOSTILE
“Isn’t it true you are in jail because you did something violent?”
KYLE ANSWER
I am in jail because I was arrested and denied release. Arrest is not conviction. I have not been to trial. Under the Fifth and Fourteenth Amendments I am presumed innocent. The State has the burden. The State has not carried it.
Q2 — HOSTILE
“Your accuser says you assaulted her. Why should anyone believe you?”
KYLE ANSWER
The record speaks. In her own writing to the District Attorney on November 8, 2025, she stated the incident “never happened.” On November 5, 2025 she asked the District Attorney to remove the protective order. She has now been credibly identified as the actual person tampering with a witness. Those are documents. Not my words. Her words.
Q3 — HOSTILE
“If you are so innocent, why did you refuse an attorney?”
KYLE ANSWER
I did not refuse an attorney. I invoked my constitutional right under Faretta v. California, 422 United States Reports 806 (1975), to represent myself. Shon Hopwood represented himself and other prisoners from Federal Correctional Institution Pekin and won a nine to zero Supreme Court reversal. Pro se is a right. Not an admission.
Q4 — HOSTILE
“Aren’t you just another veteran with post-traumatic stress disorder using his service as a shield?”
KYLE ANSWER
I am a service-connected disabled combat veteran rated one hundred percent by the Department of Veterans Affairs. My service is a fact. My combat wounds are a fact. The Silver Star and the Purple Heart are facts on a Department of Defense record. Nothing about that record is a shield. Every one of those pieces of paper was paid for with something I gave up in combat.
Q5 — HOSTILE
“Aren’t you the one tampering with witnesses right now, by giving this interview?”
KYLE ANSWER
Tampering under Texas Penal Code 36.05 is inducing a witness to lie, withhold, or absent themselves. I am speaking publicly about my case as a pretrial detainee, not contacting any witness. The First Amendment protects public speech by defendants. The Sixth Amendment guarantees a public trial. Speaking about my case on the record is the opposite of tampering.
Q6 — HOSTILE
“Why did the judge issue the initial protective order if there was nothing to it?”
KYLE ANSWER
Protective orders at the ex parte stage are issued on the affidavit of the applicant alone, before the accused is heard. That is not a finding. When both sides came before the court and the record was tested, the judge denied the extension. That is the ruling that counts.
Q7 — HOSTILE
“How long have you been in jail?”
KYLE ANSWER
More than a year, pretrial. Barker v. Wingo, 407 United States Reports 514, is the Supreme Court speedy-trial test. Under any application of that test, my case is past the constitutional floor. I have filed the demand. The record shows the demand. The delay is not my delay.
Q8 — HOSTILE
“Isn’t it a stretch to compare yourself to Shon Hopwood?”
KYLE ANSWER
Shon Hopwood was in federal prison for bank robbery. He wrote a cert petition on a prison typewriter. The Supreme Court granted cert. Nine to zero reversal. Fellers v. United States, 540 United States Reports 519. He is now a Georgetown law professor. I am a Marine Raider with a Silver Star, a Purple Heart, and a jail tablet. If he could do it from Pekin, I can do it from Travis County.
Q9 — HOSTILE
“What do you want the public to do?”
KYLE ANSWER
Read the record. The record is public. The docket is public. My motions are public. Every citation I stand on is in the United States Reports. I am not asking anyone to take my word. I am asking every American who cares about the Sixth Amendment to open the docket and read.
Q10 — HOSTILE
“Anything else, before we go?”
KYLE ANSWER
Yes. Isaiah 54:17, A Faithful Version. “No weapon that is formed against you shall prosper; and every tongue that shall rise against you in judgment you shall condemn.” That is the inheritance of the servants of the LORD. I am one of His. Semper Fidelis. He is faithful to me.
