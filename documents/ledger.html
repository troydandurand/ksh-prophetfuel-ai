  • Fourth Amendment to the United States Constitution — guarantees against unreasonable seizure; a magistration delay beyond 48 hours is presumptively unreasonable.
  • Texas Code of Criminal Procedure Article 38.23 — evidence obtained in violation of state or federal law is inadmissible in a Texas criminal trial.
